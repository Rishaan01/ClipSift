## What is AI Clipping (and Why ClipSift)?

Short-form clips are one of the fastest ways to grow views and build an audience across **YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok**. **AI clipping** takes long-form content—like podcasts, gaming sessions, and multi-hour livestreams—and automatically pulls out the funniest, sharpest, or most viral-worthy moments as ready-to-post short clips.

**The Problem:** Doing this by hand is brutal. Finding a few standout moments in a 2-to-6-hour stream means scrubbing through hours of footage, hunting for timestamps, cutting scenes one by one, reformatting them into vertical 9:16, and adding captions. On top of that, most cloud-based AI clippers lock decent exports behind expensive monthly subscriptions and watermarks.

**How ClipSift Solves It:** Paste a YouTube link and let ClipSift do the heavy lifting right on your Windows PC. It scans the video, scores the strongest highlights automatically, trims clean clips, formats them into **9:16** or **16:9**, and optionally burns in subtitles—turning hours of manual hunting and editing into a single click, completely free.

## Features

- **AI Highlight Detection** — Automatically scans long videos and completed livestreams to pick out the strongest, most engaging moments.
- **Flexible Layouts** — Export in **9:16 Blur**, **9:16 Crop**, or **Landscape 16:9** with a single click.
- **Exact Clip Control** — Pick the exact **Max Clips** you want (regardless of video length) and fine-tune the **Min AI Score** threshold to keep only top-rated moments.
- **Optional Burn-in Subtitles** — Enable subtitles when you want captions baked in, or leave them off for a super-fast pass.
- **Zero Technical Setup** — Ships as a standard Windows Setup Wizard with all required media engines bundled and automatically kept up to date.
- **Built-in YouTube Sign-In (Optional)** — Works without logging in by default; if YouTube ever asks to verify you're not a bot, connect your account in one click inside Settings.
- **Dark & Light Mode** — Clean, native desktop interface designed to stay fast and clutter-free.

## Installation

1. Go to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/Rishaan01/AiSift/releases/tag/v1.0.0)** page and download **`ClipSift_Setup.exe`**.
2. Double-click **`ClipSift_Setup.exe`** and follow the Setup Wizard.
3. Launch **ClipSift** from your Desktop or Start Menu, paste a YouTube link, and hit **Start Clipping**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Windows SmartScreen Notice:** Because ClipSift is a free, independent project without a paid code-signing certificate, Windows may show a *"Windows protected your PC"* popup on first run. Click **More info** → **Run anyway** to proceed with the installation.
